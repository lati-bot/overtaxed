# Professional Homepage Concept Comparison — September 26, 2026

## Decision
Ship an editorial / architectural hybrid of **Concept A: Review-ready case file**, rebuilt entirely in HTML/CSS with verified 6913 W Summerdale public-record facts. Use the architectural version of **Concept C: Reconstruct → Examine → Prepare** as the supporting workflow section. Reject Concept B as the homepage structure.

## Process
Nine image-model studies were generated across three concept directions and three visual treatments. They were used only for composition and art-direction exploration. No generated legal text, interface labels, values, PINs, seals, records, or agency artifacts are used in the implementation.

## Evaluation

### A. Review-ready case file — selected
**Strengths**
- Fastest category comprehension: the object reads immediately as a case file.
- Strongest evidence that Overtaxed produces a concrete professional artifact.
- Supports real source links, exclusions, adverse evidence, and unresolved questions without a dashboard aesthetic.
- Editorial variant contributes calm hierarchy and negative space.
- Architectural variant contributes restrained parcel/grid structure and technical credibility.
- Durable in responsive HTML/CSS and accessible as semantic content.

**Risks controlled in implementation**
- Avoided generated or official-looking documents.
- Replaced all placeholder text with verified case facts.
- Kept the parcel/grid layer decorative and low contrast.
- Flattened the mobile preview into a single-column review artifact.

### B. Fragmented records → inspectable file — rejected
- The transformation metaphor takes longer to understand than the case-file output.
- Funnel/ray treatments feel decorative and create illustration debt.
- Systematic warning-marker treatments read as an error state.
- Warm collage treatments emphasize the mess rather than the finished professional file.

### C. Reconstruct → Examine → Prepare — selected as support
- Excellent process legibility and explicit division of labor.
- Architectural treatment feels disciplined and remains durable in CSS.
- Too abstract to carry the hero alone, but strong directly below it.
- Rebuilt as three semantic steps with separate Overtaxed and professional responsibilities.

## Final composition
1. Direct category headline and one primary action.
2. Real-data case-file preview in the first viewport.
3. Reconstruct → Examine → Prepare workflow.
4. Inspectability principles: linked sources, visible exclusions, adverse evidence, and uncertainty.
5. Honest historical case teaser.
6. Consolidated professional-control boundary.
7. One practitioner-research invitation.

## Factual-integrity rule
Generated images remain design studies only. The shipped page uses verified text and values rendered by the application. The detailed evidence table, screening method, exclusions, open questions, and complete provenance live on the dedicated sample-case route.
