# Professional Homepage Redesign Brief — September 26, 2026

## Decision
Redesign the professional homepage around immediate category clarity and a compact, real-data product proof. Preserve the existing cream / ink / green identity, source-linked rigor and research-stage restraint. Move the full worked case and methodology out of the homepage into a dedicated sample-case page.

## Audit findings

### What the current page gets right
- It looks precise, premium and unlike an AI toy or generic SaaS template.
- The Cook County focus, real PINs, source IDs, adverse evidence and unresolved questions create unusual credibility.
- The cream / ink / green palette, restrained typography, numbered sections and documentary case-file language should remain.
- The page is technically responsive and accessible at a solid baseline: semantic headings, skip link, proper table headers, focus styles and a labelled horizontal-scroll region.
- The product posture is responsibly narrow: evidence preparation remains separate from legal judgment, filing and representation.

### What prevents first-glance understanding
- The hero headline, “Less assembly. More room for your judgment,” is elegant but does not name the product or the work.
- The visitor must infer the category from a long paragraph and then reverse-engineer the product from a detailed case.
- The full case arrives before the simple product story. The homepage becomes a methodology memo before it becomes a homepage.
- Defensive qualifications repeat often enough to compete with the value proposition.
- Technical phrases such as “record grain,” “route for documentary review,” “value-blind screening” and “comp-quality” feel like internal methodology rather than homepage language.
- The strongest proof—the inspectable case file—is not presented as a compact product visual in the first viewport.
- On mobile, the 7,491px page and horizontal evidence table make the first visit feel like reading a document rather than understanding a product.

## 3–5 second comprehension target
A first-time practitioner should be able to answer, without scrolling:
1. **Who is it for?** Cook County residential appeal professionals.
2. **What is it?** A review-first evidence-preparation workspace.
3. **What does it do?** Rebuilds public records, screens comparable candidates and organizes an inspectable case file.
4. **What remains human?** The practitioner verifies, decides, files and represents.
5. **What should I do?** View the sample case file; then talk with Overtaxed if the workflow is relevant.

## Art direction
- Editorial, evidence-led and quietly premium.
- A professional case file—not a dashboard spectacle.
- Use verified Cook County facts in HTML/CSS/SVG, never generated text or invented records.
- Preserve warm paper, near-black ink and deep civic green; add restrained warm rust only for adverse/attention states.
- Use spatial depth, paper edges, registration marks and subtle map/grid fragments sparingly.
- No gradients associated with AI, glass cards, floating dashboards, courthouse columns, gavels, handshakes or generic office portraits.
- Human warmth comes from lucid composition and authentic proof, not stock photography.

## Visual concepts to test

### A. Review-ready case file — primary candidate
A compact case-file workspace in the hero. It contains a subject header, source provenance rail, comparable shortlist, evidence labels and unresolved-question markers. Real labels and values are rendered in HTML/CSS. Generated imagery, if used, is restricted to subtle paper texture or an abstract archival background.

**Success condition:** the visual itself explains “records become one inspectable professional file.”

### B. Fragmented records → inspectable file
Restrained county-record fragments, parcel lines and tabular strips converge into one organized case file. The transformation is legible, not decorative.

**Success condition:** a new visitor understands the assembly work Overtaxed removes before reading body copy.

### C. Reconstruct → Examine → Prepare
A linear workflow diagram with three evidence states and a visible human review gate before any action.

**Success condition:** professional control is unmistakable and the diagram remains calm on mobile.

## Image-model division of labor

## Candidate evaluation rubric
Score each direction 1–5 on:
- 3-second category comprehension.
- Credibility to attorneys and appeal professionals.
- Evidence of a real product/output.
- Headline dominance and reading order.
- Mobile calmness.
- Factual integrity.
- Accessibility and contrast.
- Performance and implementation durability.
- Distinctiveness without novelty theater.

## Acceptance criteria
- The hero names Cook County, appeal evidence and professional review without scrolling.
- One primary action is visually dominant.
- The first viewport contains a credible product preview built from verified case facts.
- Homepage word count is reduced materially and the full case is preserved on a dedicated page.
- No unsupported time-savings, outcome, accuracy or legal claims.
- No generic stock photography or generated factual UI.
- No overflow at 320, 390, 600, 768, 1024 and 1440px.
- Keyboard focus, reduced motion, semantic headings and contrast remain sound.
- Build, TypeScript, targeted lint and browser interaction checks pass before commit.
